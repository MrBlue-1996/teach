## 1. Introduction

TopShelf Service LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our TopShelf Teaching platform ("Service").

**Scope**: This policy applies to all users of our Service, including individual learners, students, educators, and administrators.

## 8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

### 8.1 COPPA Compliance

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies to children under 13 in the United States.

### 8.2 School Consent

- Schools may consent on behalf of students under 13 under COPPA's "school official" exception
- Schools must have authorization from parents to provide this consent
- Schools must comply with COPPA requirements in their use of the Service

### 8.3 Parental Rights

Parents of children under 13 may:

- Review their child's personal information
- Request deletion of their child's data
- Refuse further collection
- Contact us at privacy@topshelfservice.com

### 8.4 Information Collected from Children

We limit collection from children under 13 to:

- Username and password
- Learning progress and assessment data
- Data necessary for educational purposes

We do NOT collect from children under 13:

- Personal contact information (managed by school)
- Precise geolocation
- Photos or videos
- Information for targeted advertising

## 9. Student Privacy (FERPA)

### 9.1 FERPA Compliance

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) protects student education records.

### 9.2 School Official Designation

TopShelf Service LLC operates as a "school official" under FERPA, meaning:

- We are under the direct control of the school
- We use education records only for authorized purposes
- We follow FERPA requirements for data protection

### 9.3 Education Records

Student data we process includes:

- Assessment scores and learning progress
- Competency levels and skill mastery
- Time-on-task metrics
- Behavioral data related to learning

### 9.4 Permitted Disclosures

We disclose student education records only:

- To school officials with legitimate educational interest
- With written parental/student consent
- In response to lawful subpoenas (with notice to school)
- For directory information as designated by school

### 9.5 Parent and Eligible Student Rights

Under FERPA, parents (and students 18+) have the right to:

- Inspect and review education records
- Request amendments to records
- Consent to disclosures
- File complaints with the Department of Education

### 9.6 Data Security Commitments

For student data, we:

- Encrypt all data in transit and at rest
- Limit access to authorized personnel only
- Conduct annual security assessments
- Maintain detailed audit logs
- Delete data upon school request or contract termination
