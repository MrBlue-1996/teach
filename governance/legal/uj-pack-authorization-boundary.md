# UJ Pack Authorization Boundary

## Purpose

This document defines the legal, brand, and authorization boundary for the Uncle Julio's training content pack inside TopShelf Teaching.

## Current Status

The UJ Pack is a Top Shelf Service demo-to-pilot training concept. It is not an official Uncle Julio's or Sun Holdings product unless written authorization is obtained from an authorized representative.

## Brand Boundary

The repo may contain demo references to Uncle Julio's only for internal concept, testing, and demonstration purposes. No public-facing claim may state or imply official approval, endorsement, partnership, sponsorship, or deployment unless written authorization exists.

## Content Boundary

Operationally sensitive, proprietary, or confidential Uncle Julio's content must not be committed unless the content is authorized for use. Demo content must remain clearly marked as demo, sample, synthetic, or training-only.

## Data Boundary

No real employee records, learner records, performance records, schedules, disciplinary information, payroll data, or personally identifiable information may be added to the repo for pilot work. Pilot fixtures must use synthetic sample data only.

## Approval Gate

Before any real pilot, the project must have:

1. Written authorization to use relevant brand references.
2. Written approval for any real operational content.
3. Privacy review for learner or employee data.
4. A defined pilot scope.
5. A named business owner or reviewer.
6. A no-public-claims rule until approval is documented.

## No-Go Rules

The project must not proceed to real pilot if any of these are true:

- No written authorization exists.
- Real employee data is required but privacy approval is missing.
- Content source is unclear or unauthorized.
- The demo implies official endorsement.
- The app cannot pass content-pack validation.
- The app cannot pass typecheck.
- The tracker status conflicts with documentation.
